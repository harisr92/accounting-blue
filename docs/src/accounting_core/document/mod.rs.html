1//! What every trade document shares, in any country: lines, discounts, tax and posting
2//!
3//! A document prices its [`LineItem`]s with [`price_lines`] and asks its [`TaxRegime`] for the tax
4//! on each: [`NoTax`], [`FlatRate`] for a VAT or sales tax, or a regime of your own. The result is
5//! a [`TaxBreakdown`] of named [`TaxComponent`]s, which posting turns into balanced journal
6//! entries. [`validate_document`] runs the rules every document shares.
7//!
8//! The pieces are traits, so every document type reuses them: anything that is a
9//! [`DocumentLine`] can be priced, and anything that is a [`Document`] gets the subtotal,
10//! discount and breakdown arithmetic. The sales invoice and credit note in
11//! [`sales`](crate::sales) are built this way, as is the Indian GST invoice in
12//! `gst::invoice` behind the `india` feature.

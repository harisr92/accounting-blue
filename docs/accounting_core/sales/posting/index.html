Posting sales documents: an invoice debits the receivable and credits revenue and output tax

sales_legs turns a TaxBreakdown into the legs of an invoice’s journal entry and sales_entries maps them onto the accounts named in SalesAccounts. A credit note posts the same legs on the opposite sides.

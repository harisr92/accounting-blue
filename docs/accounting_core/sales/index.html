The sales side: invoices issued to customers and credit notes against them

An Invoice bills a customer for LineItems under a TaxRegime; a CreditNote reduces an invoice, for goods returned, a discount after the sale or an amount over-billed. Both post to the ledger: an invoice debits the receivable and credits revenue and output tax, and a credit note posts the reverse.

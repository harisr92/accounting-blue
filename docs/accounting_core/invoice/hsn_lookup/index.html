HSN/SAC master data and default GST rates

The master is compiled into the crate from src/data/hsn_master.json. It covers common codes for Indian SMEs under the GST schedule named by HsnMaster::schedule, not the full CBIC tariff. Rates are defaults: some goods and services carry conditional rates (price thresholds, input tax credit options), so callers can always pass an explicit rate instead.

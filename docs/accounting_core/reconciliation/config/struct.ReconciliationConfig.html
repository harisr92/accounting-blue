Expand description

Thresholds and tolerances that control matching

The defaults suit Indian bank statements, where settlement lag of a day or two is normal and narrations are noisy. Note that with the defaults a date-shifted pair that carries no reference number lands as a PartialMatch rather than an outright match — widen date_tolerance_days or lower auto_match_threshold if you would rather accept those automatically. Pairs that agree on reference number, amount and direction always match outright, whatever their dates.

Fields§

§date_tolerance_days: i64

How many days apart two records may be dated and still score on the date dimension

§amount_tolerance_percentage: f64

Largest relative amount gap that still earns credit, as a fraction (0.01 is 1%)

§description_similarity_threshold: f64

Description similarity below which a DescriptionDifference is recorded

§auto_match_threshold: f64

Score at or above which a pair is matched without review

§partial_match_threshold: f64

Score at or above which a pair is paired up at all

§auto_resolve_threshold: f64

Score at or above which a partial match is flagged auto_resolvable

§suggestion_threshold: f64

Score at or above which a pair is offered as a suggestion for an unmatched transaction

§max_suggestions: usize

How many suggestions to keep per unmatched transaction

§candidate_window_days: i64

Pairs dated further apart than this are never scored, bounding the comparison sweep

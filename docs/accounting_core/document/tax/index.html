Tax regimes and the breakdown of tax they produce

A TaxRegime says what tax a line pays: given the line’s taxable value and its rate, it returns a TaxBreakdown of named TaxComponents. NoTax charges nothing and FlatRate charges one tax at the line’s rate, such as VAT or a sales tax. The Indian GST regime, which splits a rate into CGST + SGST or charges IGST, lives in gst::tax behind the india feature.

Regimes are pure: they take values and return values, and the same input always gives the same breakdown.

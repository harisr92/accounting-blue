Document lines: what is supplied, how many, at what price and tax rate

A DocumentLine is anything priced as quantity × unit price less a Discount, at a tax rate. LineItem is the plain line this module ships; the Indian GstLineItem is another DocumentLine that also carries an HSN/SAC code. check_line holds the rules every line must pass.

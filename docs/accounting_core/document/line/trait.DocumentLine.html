fn taxable_value(&self) -> BigDecimal

Taxable value of the line: quantity × unit price less the line’s discount, exactly

This is not rounded, and is before any share of a document discount; Document::line_tax_breakdowns takes that share off and rounds, so its taxable_value is the amount that is invoiced and posted.

Module document 

Source
Expand description

What every trade document shares, in any country: lines, discounts, tax and posting

A document prices its LineItems with price_lines and asks its TaxRegime for the tax on each: NoTax, FlatRate for a VAT or sales tax, or a regime of your own. The result is a TaxBreakdown of named TaxComponents, which posting turns into balanced journal entries. validate_document runs the rules every document shares.

The pieces are traits, so every document type reuses them: anything that is a DocumentLine can be priced, and anything that is a Document gets the subtotal, discount and breakdown arithmetic. The sales invoice and credit note in sales are built this way, as is the Indian GST invoice in gst::invoice behind the india feature.

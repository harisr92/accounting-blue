Who issues a GST document: the seller’s registration

A SellerRegistration says whether the seller may charge GST. A regular taxpayer issues tax invoices. A composition taxable person (Section 10 of the CGST Act) and a seller not registered under GST may not collect tax, so what they issue is a bill of supply (Rule 49 of the CGST Rules): no tax on any line, and, for a composition seller, the declaration Rule 5(1)(g) asks for. A composition seller still pays tax on its own turnover, at its CompositionRate.

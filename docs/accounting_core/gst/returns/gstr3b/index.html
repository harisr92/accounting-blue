GSTR-3B’s outward supplies: Tables 3.1 and 3.2 from the period’s invoices and credit notes

GSTR-3B is the monthly summary return a regular taxpayer files with the tax paid. Table 3.1 reports the month’s outward supplies in five rows; this builds the four that sales decide:

  • (a) taxable supplies other than zero-rated, nil-rated and exempt: the lines that charge GST;
  • (b) zero-rated supplies (exports and supplies to SEZs): none, since exports aren’t covered;
  • (c) other outward supplies, nil-rated and exempt: taxable lines at 0% and exempt lines;
  • (d) inward supplies liable to reverse charge: purchases, not built from sales, so zero;
  • (e) non-GST outward supplies.

Table 3.2 breaks the inter-state part of row (a) down by place of supply for supplies to unregistered persons, composition taxable persons and UIN holders. Only the first is built: the crate has no composition or UIN-holder buyers.

A credit note is subtracted from the row its lines fall in. Bills of supply, and credit notes against them, are left out: their sellers, under composition or not registered, don’t file GSTR-3B. Building is pure, and the documents are checked as Gstr1Return::build checks them. Gstr3bOutward::to_json writes the result in the GST portal’s GSTR-3B schema for upload. It leaves out the tables sales don’t decide: Table 3.1.1 (supplies through an e-commerce operator), Table 4 (input tax credit), Table 5 (exempt inward supplies), Table 5.1 (interest and late fee) and Table 6 (payment of tax).

Rows are written as summed. A month whose credit notes outweigh its sales makes a row negative; the portal refuses negative values, so the filer adjusts those figures by hand.
